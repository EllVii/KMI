Bridging the gap between crisis and stability.

KMI works as a referral agency and community resource center, helping connect people with compassionate service and trusted community pathways.

🏠

Housing

KMI helps unhoused individuals, veterans, and community members identify housing-related resources and referral pathways toward a safer, more stable place to live.

🥫

Food Distribution

KMI supports neighbors facing hunger through food-relief outreach and connections to supportive community relationships and services.

🎓

Scholarship Association

Scholarship support is intended to help underserved and marginalized prospective students pursue higher education through funding, mentorship, and resource connections.

🧭

Life Coaching

Structured guidance can help participants identify goals, navigate transitions, build resilience, and work toward greater self-sufficiency.

🙏

Biblical Counseling

Christ-centered instruction and guidance for couples, individuals, and families seeking faith-based direction.
