What happens after you choose a path?

The goal is to make the next step predictable without promising an outcome that depends on program availability or the nature of the request.

1️⃣

Choose the closest reason

Select help, volunteering, partnership, education, or general contact. The email subject is prepared for you.

2️⃣

Share the useful details

Include enough information for KMI to understand your question, organization, need, or interest without sending highly sensitive information by ordinary email.

3️⃣

KMI reviews the request

KMI can then respond with the appropriate next step, information, conversation, or referral based on the request and current availability.
