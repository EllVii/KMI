What KMI is preparing

The planned crypto pathway will use a third-party charitable giving provider so the provider can handle supported assets, transaction instructions, donor documentation, conversion, and distribution through its secure process.

  • No private wallet keys will be stored on the KMI website.
  • No public KMI wallet address will be posted for direct transfers.
  • The provider-specific link will be published only after KMI completes setup and verification.

Giving today

KMI's Givebutter online giving pathway is available now for approved online donations. Cryptocurrency is a future option and is intentionally kept separate until the provider connection is ready.
