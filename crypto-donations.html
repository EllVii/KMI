Cryptocurrency Giving

KMI’s cryptocurrency pathway is designed to use an approved third-party charitable donation provider rather than asking donors to send funds to wallet addresses posted directly on this website.

Why use a third-party provider?

An established charitable giving provider can handle supported cryptocurrency selection, transaction instructions, conversion, donor documentation, and distribution to the nonprofit through its own secure process.

What KMI does not ask you to do

KMI does not ask visitors to copy cryptocurrency wallet keys or send funds to wallet addresses published directly on this public website.
