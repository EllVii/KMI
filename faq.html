What is Kingdom Missions International?

Kingdom Missions International, also known as KMI, is a faith-centered Christian ministry based in Las Vegas, Nevada. KMI focuses on community resources, education, referrals, outreach, volunteering, partnerships, and local and international mission support.

Where does KMI serve?

KMI is based in Las Vegas and describes service in Las Vegas, Los Angeles, the Philippines, and other communities where ministry opportunities develop. Its website organizes local resource help separately from broader outreach and mission activity.

What community resources does KMI help with?

KMI provides guidance and referral pathways related to housing, food support, scholarships, life coaching, biblical counseling, and other community resources. The Resources page explains each area, while KMI Connect is the starting point for a request.

Does KMI provide education opportunities?

KMI presents faith-based education and certification pathways that may include religion, business, chaplaincy, biblical counseling, and other service-centered learning. Visitors should contact KMI for current availability and program details.

How can I volunteer with KMI?

Volunteers can express interest in food distribution, community outreach, fellowship, teaching, administration, or mission support. KMI Connect provides a dedicated volunteer path so the inquiry is clear from the beginning.

Volunteer With KMI

How can an organization partner with KMI?

Churches, ministries, nonprofits, schools, businesses, educators, and community programs can contact KMI about cooperative service and outreach. Partnership inquiries have a dedicated path on KMI Connect.

Partner With KMI

How can I support KMI financially?

KMI publishes current giving information on its Give page and keeps payment processing with approved third-party providers. If a donation method is not active, visitors are directed to contact KMI rather than being sent to a broken payment flow.

View Giving Options

How do I contact Kingdom Missions International?

Use the KMI Connect page or email info@KingdomMissionsGlobal.org. Connect separates resource help, volunteering, partnerships, education, and general inquiries so visitors do not have to guess where to start.
