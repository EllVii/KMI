Website Privacy

This page explains the public website's current data flow in plain language. It does not replace the privacy terms of third-party services used for donations or email.

Public website

KMI's public website is primarily informational. The current public pages do not ask visitors to create an account or enter card, bank, or cryptocurrency credentials into KMI-hosted forms.

Email contact

When you choose an email contact link, your message is handled by your email provider and the recipient's email provider. Do not send highly sensitive information by ordinary email.

Online donations

KMI uses Givebutter for approved online giving. Payment information entered into Givebutter is processed through Givebutter's donation experience rather than stored by the KMI public website.

Website infrastructure

KMI's website infrastructure may be delivered through hosting and security providers such as Cloudflare and GitHub during the transition to KMI's permanent hosting setup. Those providers may process technical request data needed to deliver and secure the site.

Questions

Ask before sharing sensitive information.

If you are unsure whether information should be sent through email, a donation provider, or a future KMI support system, contact KMI first.

Privacy inquiry

Questions about the public website or current contact pathways can be sent to KMI.
