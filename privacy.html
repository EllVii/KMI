Website Privacy

This page explains the current KMI public website data flow in plain language, including the optional secure inquiry form, email contact, donation processing, and infrastructure providers.

Reviewed: November 5, 2026

Public website

KMI's public website is primarily informational. Visitors do not need a public KMI account to browse ministry information, resources, education, outreach, fellowship, giving information, or contact pathways.

Optional online inquiry

When KMI's secure inquiry form is enabled, it may collect your name, email address, inquiry topic, message, and consent to use that information to respond. The form is hidden when the secure integration is not available.

Please do not submit financial account numbers, identification documents, medical histories, passwords, or emergency information.

Security verification

The optional inquiry form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help reduce automated abuse. The website verifies the Turnstile result before forwarding a valid inquiry to KMI's private follow-up system.

Private follow-up workspace

Validated online inquiries are forwarded server-to-server to KMI's private CRM follow-up workspace. They are not published on the public website. KMI uses the submitted information to review and respond to the inquiry.

Email contact

If the online form is unavailable, or if you prefer email, the website provides direct email links. Messages sent by email are handled by your email provider and the recipient's email provider rather than the website inquiry bridge.

Online donations

KMI uses Givebutter for approved online giving. Payment details entered into Givebutter are processed through Givebutter's donation experience and are not stored by KMI's public website.

Website infrastructure

KMI's website may be delivered through providers such as Cloudflare and GitHub. Those providers may process technical request information needed to deliver, secure, monitor, and troubleshoot the website.

Retention and privacy requests

The public website does not publish a universal retention period for every inquiry or provider record. If you have a question about information you submitted, correction, deletion, or the current handling process, contact KMI directly so the request can be reviewed in context.

Use the right channel

Do not use the website as an emergency service.

For urgent safety, medical, housing, or food emergencies, contact the appropriate emergency service or established local provider directly.

Privacy or website question

Questions about the public website, the inquiry form, or current contact pathways can be sent to KMI.
